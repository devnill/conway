### Fixed

- **The one-shot deadline error now names the deadline it blew, not just how long the run took** — board item `01M2RDGVXFJ09DE9GM982HC23A`. `oneshot.rs` printed `no response from <backend> within {elapsed}s (deadline exceeded)`, where `{elapsed}` is `turn_started.elapsed()` — the run's own duration, *not* the configured limit. A run that died after well under a second therefore reported `within 0s`, which every reader parses as "the deadline was zero seconds" rather than "zero seconds went by". The message now carries the real `deadline=<instant>` (`ResultStatus::BudgetExceeded::limit`, as composed by `supervisor::budget_exceeded` and `AgentLoop::check_budget`) alongside the elapsed time. This matters most in the case the old wording actively hid: an elapsed time far *below* the limit means the deadline was already spent before the turn began — a different failure from a slow or unreachable backend, and one the operator would otherwise chase in the wrong place.
