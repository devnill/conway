### Fixed

- **A turn that exhausts the model's own output token budget while still reasoning now says so, instead of leaving the transcript silent** — board item `01M23JRDAM480SRXFGM46GBVA6`. A real dogfooding incident: a turn's response came back `stop: max_tokens` with a single, 32k-character `Thinking` block and nothing else — no text, no tool call — and conway showed nothing at all; the operator had to ask "what just happened?" to find out. `AgentLoop` now recognizes a turn that ends `stop: max_tokens` having produced neither a `Text` nor a `ToolUse` block, and appends a `SystemNote` (and a live `Event::AgentProgress` twin, so a watching TUI sees it the instant the turn ends, not only on the next resume) naming exactly that: the output budget ran out while the model was still reasoning, no answer was produced, the reasoning itself is preserved in the log above — and offering retry, raising the cap, or asking something narrower, never doing any of those automatically. A turn that hit the same cap but had already said something, or already proposed a tool call, gets separate, distinguishing wording ("cut off mid-answer") rather than the same "said nothing" note; an ordinary completed turn gets neither.
