### Fixed

- **A SIGTERM-ignoring backgrounded process could survive `bash`/hook-runner/plugin teardown entirely** — board item `01M201DS6CZ7F3AX7N3P865VWX`. `kill_group`'s (`conway_tools::process::unix::kill_group`) SIGTERM-then-SIGKILL grace window used to be timed against the group *leader's* own exit; under non-interactive `bash -c` a backgrounded `cmd &` grandchild shares the leader's process group but not its lifetime, so a leader that exited quickly (the common shape) made the grace check resolve before the rest of the group was ever observed, and SIGKILL was never sent. The grace window now polls the whole process group and escalates to SIGKILL only once no member remains alive or the 2s grace period elapses, whichever comes first — the returned `ExitStatus` and every call site are unchanged.
