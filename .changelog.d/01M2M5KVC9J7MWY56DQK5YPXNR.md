### Fixed

- **A crash right after an atomic sidecar write could still lose the rename, even though the file's bytes were durably flushed** — board item `01M2M5KVC9J7MWY56DQK5YPXNR`. The "write a temp sibling, `fsync` it, rename over the target" protocol existed twice (`conway-tools`' `write` tool and `conway`'s own facade wrapper), and neither copy `fsync`ed `path`'s parent directory after the `rename` — the step that makes the rename's directory-entry update itself durable, not just the bytes it points at. There is now exactly one implementation, `conway_tools::fs::write::atomic_write`, which does. `conway::fs::atomic_write` (used by `conway-cli`'s `sessions name`/history persistence and `conway-plugin-names`) is now a thin sync wrapper over it and is gated `#[cfg(feature = "builtin-tools")]` — its only implementation lives in the optional, default-on `conway-tools` crate.
