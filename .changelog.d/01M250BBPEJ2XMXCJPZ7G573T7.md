### Fixed

- **`--model` is no longer silently dropped when combined with `--fork-from`** — board item F3 (`01M250BBPEJ2XMXCJPZ7G573T7`). Previously a `--model` pin was ignored on this arm and the failure surfaced three layers downstream as a routing error (`no candidate for role default (0 considered)`) naming the wrong cause. Two things were missing, not one. The `--fork-from` dispatch arm in `oneshot::resolve_session` never called `parse_model_pin`, so the pin never reached the `ForkSpec`; and `Conway::fork_from` mapped six of `AgentKnobs`' seven fields into `ForkChildRequest`, dropping `model` on the floor, so even a pin set directly by an embedder through `ForkSpec::model` was discarded before it reached `ResumeSpec`. Both are fixed: the arm now reads `cli.model` through `parse_model_pin` exactly as the flag-free, `--session` and `--resume` arms already did, and `ForkChildRequest` carries the seventh knob through to the child's `ResumeSpec`. The pin applies per invocation, as it does on every other arm — `SessionMeta` carries no pin field, so a later `Conway::resume` of the forked child resolves its model afresh. `docs/scripting.md`'s flag table now states that `--model` composes with both `--fork-from` and `--resume`.
