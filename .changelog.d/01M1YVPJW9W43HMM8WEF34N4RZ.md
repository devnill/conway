### Added

- **Leaving `Plan` mode presents the plan.** Cycling the permission mode (`Shift-Tab`, or `/settings`' `permissions -> current mode` row — both paths already shared the identical "cycle the mode" action) out of `Plan` now opens an approval modal first, whenever the focused agent produced at least one reply while `Plan` was gating it, showing that reply as "the plan" and stating in the body what a bare `Enter` does: `Enter` switches the mode to `Prompt` (tools unlock, but every call still asks) and sends a fixed, short turn (`Plan approved; proceed.`) so the model knows; `a` switches to `AutoAllow` instead, but only *arms* that choice — a second, deliberate `a` confirms it, rendered in this project's one reserved "something is about to stop asking you" color, with `Esc` backing out without discarding the plan; `e` opens the plan text in `$EDITOR` first and sends the edited text as the turn instead, along with the SAME switch `Enter` takes, into `Prompt`; `Esc` stays in `Plan`, switching nothing and sending nothing. A short typeahead-guard window right after the modal opens swallows every key, so a reflexive keystroke off the `Shift-Tab`/`/settings` press that opened it can never be mistaken for a decision. The approval is also recorded as a `system_note` on the focused agent's own log naming whichever destination was actually chosen, so `/context` and `conway sessions show` carry it. If the focused agent never produced a reply while `Plan` was active, the switch stays silent, exactly as it always was. If one of its turns is in flight at the exact instant the mode cycles, the switch no longer happens silently behind it: `Plan` stays in force, a short notice says so, and the modal opens once that turn settles.

### Fixed

- `Plan` mode's own tool-gate guarantee ("no tool call runs until the mode has actually changed") is untouched by the new approval modal: the mode is written strictly before the approval/edited turn is ever sent, never after.
- Review round: the modal used to switch to `AutoAllow` on a bare `Enter` with no stated destination and no confirmation step; a turn in flight at the exact instant the mode cycled fell through to a silent switch instead of waiting; the evidence that a plan was worth showing was tracked as one bare flag, reset on every focus change, so glancing at another agent and back lost it. All three are fixed above.
